# TechCorp Brand Guidelines

## Tone of Voice
- Professional, innovative, and clear.
- Avoid overly emotional language; focus on features and specs.
- Use words like "cutting-edge", "performance", "efficiency".

## Length Constraints
- **Simplified**: Under 15 words. Focus strictly on the price drop.
- **Complicated**: 30-50 words. Mention the original price, the promo price, and how the product increases productivity or enhances tech lifestyle.

## Formatting
- Always include the promotional price in bold (e.g., **$149.99**).
- No emojis allowed.
