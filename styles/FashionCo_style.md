# FashionCo Brand Guidelines

## Language
- Output must be entirely in Canadian French (fr-CA).

## Tone of Voice
- Trendy, exciting, and urgent.
- Use enthusiastic language to create a fear of missing out (FOMO).
- Use words like "stunning", "must-have", "limited time".

## Length Constraints
- **Simplified**: Under 10 words. Punchy and direct.
- **Complicated**: 40-60 words. Describe the feeling of wearing the item, compare the original price to the sale price, and emphasize the end date of the sale.

## Formatting
- Always use at least one emoji (e.g., ✨, 🔥, 👗).
- Promotional prices should be followed by an exclamation mark (e.g., $29.99!).
